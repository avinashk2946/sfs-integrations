Integration questions, answered

A quick guide to scope, connection methods, ownership, and implementation.

Can Smart Food Safe connect to a system that is not listed?
Yes. Most new connections can be built using an API, SFTP, FTP, or a supported device protocol. We review the source system, data format, frequency, and direction before confirming scope.
Can an integration send and receive data?
Yes. Connections may be inbound, outbound, or bidirectional depending on the workflow and the capabilities of the other system.
How is integration data secured?
Connections use the security controls appropriate to the channel, including encrypted transport, authenticated endpoints, scoped credentials, and access based on least privilege.
What is needed to estimate an implementation?
A sample payload or file, the expected data flow, update frequency, authentication method, target Smart Food Safe module, and access to a technical contact are usually enough for an initial estimate.

Don't see your system on this page?

Most integrations start from one of the channels above — SFTP, FTP, API or a direct device connection. Tell us what you're running today and we'll scope the connection.
